Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026 · Version 1.0

Teumnyang keeps your plans, notes, checklists, focus records and cat decorating progress only on your device, with no sign-up. This policy explains how LogicPartners (로직파트너스, “we”), the operator of the Teumnyang app (the “App”), handles personal information under the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea.

1. Information we handle and why

InformationPurposeWhere it's stored
The name (nickname) you choose and your catGreetings and displayYour device
Plan titles, dates, times, reminders, repeat rules, notes and checklistsPlanning and remindersYour device
Focus records, Nyang Star and treat history, room decorationsCalculating rewards, preventing duplicate rewards, decoratingYour device
A summary of today's plans for widgetsHome Screen and Lock Screen widgetsYour device (shared App Group area)
Diary entries, moods, stickers and one photo you chooseDaily records, making share cardsYour device (the photo is resized on your device)
Alarm times, days, labels and soundsAlarmsYour device (on iOS 26 or later, scheduled as a system alarm)

We never send this information to a server, and we can't see it. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

The App only receives the one photo you pick in the system photo picker and doesn't access the rest of your library. When you save a diary card to Photos, the App uses only the “Add Photos” permission.

2. Payment information

Teumnyang PLUS purchases (monthly, yearly and lifetime) are processed by the Apple App Store (and by Google Play when the Android version launches). We never receive card numbers or other payment details; the App only uses the subscription or purchase status that Apple confirms on your device. Refunds follow each store's policy.

3. Ads and external links (applies only to versions that turn these features on)

The current version shows no ads and contains no affiliate links.

  • Rewarded ads: We may use Google AdMob for a feature that gives you treats for watching an ad to the end. In that case, information such as your device's advertising identifier may be shared with Google to serve the ad. On iOS we ask for App Tracking Transparency permission first, and if you don't allow it, only non-personalized ads are shown.
  • Coupang Partners links: If you tap a product recommendation link in the App, you'll go to the Coupang website, where Coupang's privacy policy applies. We don't receive information that identifies you through these links.

4. Retention and deletion

Information stored on your device is deleted when you delete the App. Backup files you make with “Create a backup file” in the App (diary photos aren't included) are kept wherever you choose, and you can delete them yourself.

5. Sharing and outsourcing

We don't provide your personal information to third parties or outsource its processing. Store payments and the ad feature described in sections 2 and 3 are handled by those companies under their own policies.

6. Sharing plans with friends (not available yet — this policy will be updated first)

The App doesn't offer a way to show your plans to friends yet. Before we open such a feature, we'll first publish in this policy what is stored on a server, how long it's kept and how to delete it, and we'll ask for your consent in the App.

7. Your rights

You can edit or delete your plans, notes and records in the App at any time, and you can erase all of your information by deleting the App. Please send any questions to the contact below.

8. Children

The App works without sign-up or any collection on a server. In versions with ads turned on, we won't show personalized ads to users identified as under 14.

9. Security

App data is protected by the iOS app sandbox and device encryption. We don't put secret keys for outside services in the App.
