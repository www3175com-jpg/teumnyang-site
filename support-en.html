Frequently asked questions

My reminders don't go off.

Turn on Allow Notifications in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Teumnyang. If a Focus such as Do Not Disturb is on, notifications may arrive silently. A plan needs a set time to have a reminder.

Will my records move to a new phone?

Teumnyang keeps your records only on your device. Before you switch, tap “Create a backup file” in the Me tab, then use “Restore from a backup” on the new phone. If you move to the new phone with an iCloud device backup, your records come along too.

I bought PLUS, but it isn't working.

Tap “Restore purchases” in Me › Teumnyang PLUS. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account. You can request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I get more Nyang Stars?

You get 2 for completing a plan, 3 for each of your Top 3, and 10 more for finishing all three (there's a daily limit). Finishing a focus session also gives you 1 star for every 5 minutes.

I can't hear the focus timer sounds.

The default scene is a quiet one with no sound. In Rain, Café, Forest and Campfire, tap the speaker button at the top right to turn the sound on or off. Sounds you turn on play even in Silent mode. Please check your volume too.

My alarm only vibrates, with no sound.

On iOS 26 or later, alarms ring as system alarms with sound even in Silent mode. Allow the Alarms permission when you create your first alarm (you can also change it in the Teumnyang section of iPhone Settings). Below iOS 26, or if the permission is off, alarms ring as notifications, which may only vibrate in Silent mode. To make sure you don't miss them, Teumnyang reminds you 4 more times, a minute apart.
