Frequently asked questions

My reminders don't go off.

Turn on Allow Notifications in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Teumnyang. If a Focus such as Do Not Disturb is on, notifications may arrive silently. A plan needs a set time to have a reminder.

Will my records move to a new phone?

Teumnyang keeps your records only on your device. Before you switch, tap “Create a backup file” in the Me tab, then use “Restore from a backup” on the new phone. If you move to the new phone with an iCloud device backup, your records come along too.

I bought PLUS, but it isn't working.

Tap “Restore purchases” in Me › Teumnyang PLUS. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account. You can request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I get more Nyang Stars?

You get 2 for completing a plan, 3 for a promise, and 10 more for keeping all three of today's promises (there's a daily limit). Finishing a focus session also gives you 1 star for every 5 minutes.

I can't hear the focus timer sounds.

If your iPhone is in Silent mode, scene sounds don't play either. The default scene is a quiet one with no sound; in Rain, Café, Forest and Campfire, tap the speaker button at the top right to turn the sound on or off.
